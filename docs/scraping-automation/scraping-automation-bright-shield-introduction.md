# Get to know Bright Shield

> **Official Source**: [https://docs.brightdata.com/scraping-automation/bright-shield/introduction](https://docs.brightdata.com/scraping-automation/bright-shield/introduction)
> **Category**: `scraping-automation`

---

Bright Shield is Bright Data's risk management suite for ensuring web scraping activities are conducted legally, ethically, and in a compliant manner.

Bright-Shield is a risk management suite of solutions, ensures scraping activities are being done legally, ethically and in a compliant manner, protecting both the business doing the scraping, and the target website.
