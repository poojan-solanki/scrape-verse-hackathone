# Bright Data promo codes

> **Official Source**: [https://docs.brightdata.com/general/account/billing-and-pricing/promo-codes](https://docs.brightdata.com/general/account/billing-and-pricing/promo-codes)
> **Category**: `general`

---

Find and apply Bright Data promo codes in 3 steps to get exclusive discounts on subscription plans, products and services across the platform.

Bright Data offers promo codes to save money on your subscription plans. You can get exclusive discounts on specific products or services by applying a promo code. This guide will show you everything you need to know about the promo codes.

## How to Apply a Promo Code

To apply a promo code to your subscription plan, follow the steps below:

### Step 1. Go to the Billing page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Bright Data website">
    Visit [Bright Data](https://brightdata.com/cp/start) page and sign in to your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the &#x22;Billing&#x22; page">
    Click on the [Billing](https://brightdata.com/cp/billing) page on the left navigation bar.

    <Frame>
      <img alt="billing-button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2. Apply the Promo Code

You can choose to apply the promo code in one of two ways:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Pay as you go plan">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click on &#x22;Apply a promo code&#x22;">
        <Frame>
          <img alt="click-on-apply-promo-code" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enter the Promo Code">
        To activate the promotion, enter your code in the “Enter promo code” field, then click “Apply.”

        <Frame>
          <img alt="enter-promo-code" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Congratulations 🎉">
        You have successfully activated the promo code, and your new discounted rates have been applied to your plan.

        <Frame>
          <img alt="payg-promo-added.png" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="View Updated Pricing">
        You can confirm the changes by going to the “Overview” tab on the “Billing” page, under “Your products” > “All Products,” to view the discounted prices for the relevant product(s). Or you can simply open a zone and get started!

        <Frame>
          <img alt="payg-confirm-discount" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Subscription plan">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click on &#x22;Change Plan&#x22;">
        In [Overview](https://brightdata.com/cp/billing/overview) tab, click on the [Change Plan](https://brightdata.com/cp/pricing_packages) button.

        <Frame>
          <img alt="change-plan" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Select the Subscription">
        Select the subscription plan of your choice and click on the "Upgrade" button.

        <Frame>
          <img alt="subscription-plans" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enter the Promo Code">
        To activate the promotion, enter your code in the “Enter promo code” field, then click “Apply.” Once you see confirmation of the promo code’s validity, click “Pay & Confirm Upgrade."

        <Frame>
          <img alt="apply-promo-code" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Congratulations 🎉">
        You have successfully activated the promo code, and your new discounted rates have been applied to your plan.

        <Frame>
          <img alt="sub-promo-added.png" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>

      <Step title="View Updated Pricing">
        You can confirm the changes by going to the “Overview” tab on the “Billing” page, under “Your products” > “All Products,” to view the discounted prices for the relevant product(s). Or you can simply open a zone and get started!

        <Frame>
          <img alt="sub-confirm-discount" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do the promo codes work with the 'dollar-for-dollar' first deposit matching promo?">
    Yes, you can use both a promo code and the dollar-for-dollar matching promotion together.

    The promo code will be applied directly to your subscription plan, reducing the cost of your plan. Additionally, we will match your first deposit dollar-for-dollar, up to \$500. The matched promotional credits are valid for 90 days. To maintain the bonus, a minimum monthly usage of \$5 is required.

    <Note>
      The matching dollar for dollar credit works for the first deposit only.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
