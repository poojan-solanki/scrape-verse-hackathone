## About JNPA
Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (JNPA) at Navi Mumbai is India's premier container handling Port.
- Accounts for ~50% of total containerised cargo volume across major ports of India
- Commissioned: 26th May 1989
- Ranked 23rd among top 100 Container Ports in the world
- Connected to 200+ ports worldwide
